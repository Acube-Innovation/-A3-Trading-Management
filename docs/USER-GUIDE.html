A3 Trading Management · user guide

How the system works, for the people who will use it

A3 buys material, builds trailers, gives each one a chassis number, keeps it in stock, sells it and delivers it. This guide follows one trailer along that road and shows which screen you use at each point, which button to press, and what you should see.

1 · The system in one minute

Everything in the system hangs on one idea: a trailer is not a quantity, it is an individual. The moment A3 decides to build one, it receives its own chassis number, and that number follows it for the rest of its life — onto the plate in the fabrication bay, into the stock list, onto the delivery note, onto the invoice, and onto its warranty record afterwards. You never sell "3 flatbeds"; you sell chassis A3-26-000296.

A trailer's life in the system looks like this:

Buymaterial comes in from suppliers
Builda work order issues the chassis numbers
StagesMaterial → Fabrication → Assembly → Paint → QC → Delivery
Stockthe finished trailer sits in the Finished Goods yard
Sellan order reserves a specific chassis
Deliverthe delivery note and invoice carry the chassis
Warrantystart and expiry live on the same number

Every screen you will use is on the left-hand menu of the portal — eight entries. The buying, selling and accounts screens are grouped behind one of them, Transactions.

2 · Where you work

There are two places, and it helps to know which is which.

The portal

The screens on the left-hand menu (Home, Customers, Manufacturing, Serial Register, Chassis Plates, Suppliers, Transactions, Live Stock). This is where daily work happens: lists of what is waiting, the buttons that move things forward, and the numbers that tell you where you stand. It is designed to be used all day without training.

The standard forms

Whenever you press New … or Open on a portal list, the full entry form for that document opens in a new tab. This is the standard ERPNext form — every field is available there. You fill in the details, press Save, close the tab and come back to the portal list, where the document now appears and can be approved. The forms look busier than the portal; you only need the fields this guide mentions.

Draft, then approved. Almost every document has two lives. When you save it, it is a draft: you can still change or delete it, and it has no effect on stock or money. When someone presses Approve (the standard forms call the same step Submit), it is posted: it now counts — stock moves, money is owed — and it can no longer be edited. To undo a posted document you cancel it and, if needed, make an amended copy. This is the safety net of the whole system: nothing counts until a person approves it.

4 · Before the first day

Five things must be in place before the cycle below will run. The first three are deliberate locks: the system refuses to guess them.

  1. Confirm the chassis number format. In the standard forms, open Chassis Numbering Rule. Set the prefix (for example A3), whether the year is included and how (26 or 2026), the separator, how many digits the running number has, the number to start from, and whether a check digit is added. The form shows the next number it would issue, e.g. A3-26-000297. When it is right, tick Format Confirmed by Redlines. Until that box is ticked, no chassis number can be issued and a trailer work order is refused. This is on purpose — the number is embossed into steel and cannot be taken back.
  2. Mark the trailer models. Open each trailer model in Item and tick Is a Trailer. Only items with this tick receive chassis numbers. Parts and raw material stay unticked, so a work order for them is an ordinary work order.
  3. Load supplier papers. On each Supplier, enter the expiry dates of the trade licence, the VAT certificate and the establishment card, and set the compliance status. A purchase order to a supplier whose papers have lapsed is refused, so real dates must be in before buying starts. The Suppliers screen shows who is Clear, Expiring (within 30 days) or Blocked.
  4. Give each trailer model a parts list. A work order needs a BOM (bill of materials — the list of items and operations that make one trailer). Without an active BOM the system says "No active BOM found" and will not build.
  5. Prices and places. Buying and selling prices on the items (an order with no price produces a bill of zero, which cannot be paid), and the three yards: Stores (raw material), Work In Progress (on the floor) and Finished Goods (ready to sell). The company, warehouses, price lists and units were set up during installation and should be checked against how A3 actually works.

5 · The complete cycle

This is the road one trailer takes, in the order the office will do it. Use it as a walkthrough with the client, and as the manual test: each step says where you are, what to press, what you should see, and what would stop you. All of these screens except Manufacturing sit under Transactions.

Buy

from a request for material to paying the supplier · 5 steps

  1. Ask for material — a requisition

    Screen: Transactions › Requisitions · creates MAT-MR-2026-00022
    Do

    Press New Requisition. The form opens: add the items and quantities you need, and the yard they should go to (usually Stores). Save and return to the list.

    Then

    The requisition shows as Draft with three buttons. Approve (asks "Approve requisition …?") marks it approved; Reject removes it with a reason; To Order asks which supplier and turns it into a purchase order.

    See

    The top of the screen counts Requisitions, Awaiting Approval and Approved. Live Stock's "Request Selected" button raises requisitions from low-stock alerts the same way.

  2. Order it — a purchase order

    Screen: Transactions › Purchase Orders · creates PUR-ORD-2026-00022
    Do

    The order made by To Order appears as a draft. Press Open to check the prices (a requisition carries no price, so the order takes the item's buying price; if there is none, type the rate in). Then Approve — "Approve purchase order …?". You can also start from scratch with New Order.

    See

    Status changes to To Receive and Bill. Order Value at the top adds up approved orders. A Receive button appears on the row.

    Stops you

    "Purchase Order cannot be raised for <supplier>: lapsed: trade licence …" — the supplier's papers have expired or their status is not compliant. Update the supplier record first. This check runs everywhere an order is made, including the standard form.

  3. Receive it — a goods receipt

    Screen: Transactions › Goods Receipt · creates MAT-PRE-2026-00021
    Do

    Orders not yet received are listed under Awaiting Receipt. Press Receive ("Create a draft goods receipt against …?"). A draft receipt is made with the ordered quantities; Edit it if fewer arrived or some were rejected. When the material is physically in, press Submit — the warning says it: "This posts the stock."

    See

    The quantity is now in the Stores yard: Live Stock shows it under Stocked (Stock In) and in Stock by Warehouse. The receipt row gains a Bill button.

  4. Get the bill — a purchase invoice, with the hold

    Screen: Transactions › Purchase Invoices · creates ACC-PINV-2026-00018
    Do

    Press Bill on the receipt ("Raise a purchase invoice against …?"). The bill appears as a draft. If anything about it needs checking, press Hold and type the reason; the row shows On Hold. When it is cleared, Release, then Approve — "This posts it to the ledger."

    See

    Once approved, the amount appears in Outstanding at the top of the screen and under Owed by Us on Payments & Receipts. A Pay button appears.

    Stops you

    "… is on hold. Release the hold before approving it." The hold is A3's own gate — it keeps a questionable bill out of the books entirely, which the standard system cannot do (its own hold only works after a bill has posted).

  5. Pay the supplier

    Screen: Transactions › Purchase Invoices, then Supplier Payments · creates ACC-PAY-2026-00013
    Do

    Press Pay on the approved bill. It shows the outstanding amount and asks how much to pay — accept it, or type a smaller amount for a part payment. A draft payment is made. On Supplier Payments, press Submit ("Submit payment …?") once the money has actually gone.

    See

    Owed to Suppliers drops by the amount; the supplier's balance is shown by age (how long each bill has been waiting). The bill's status becomes Paid.

    Stops you

    "… has nothing outstanding to pay. Check the rates on the order" — the bill totalled zero because the order had no prices. Fix the prices on the order before billing.

Build

a work order issues the chassis numbers; six stages take it into stock · 3 steps

  1. Raise the work order — chassis numbers are issued now

    Screen: Manufacturing · creates MFG-WO-2026-00014 and, for 2 trailers, chassis A3-26-000296 and A3-26-000288
    Do

    Press New. Search the trailer model (only items with a BOM are offered), set the quantity, the planned start and target delivery dates, which yard raw material is taken from (Stores) and where finished trailers go (Finished Goods). If a customer has already ordered, pick their order under For sales order; otherwise leave it as Build to stock. Write the specification for the floor — colour, axles, options — it prints on the traveler. Then Create Work Order.

    The material check. As soon as the BOM and quantity are set, the form shows each raw material with what the order needs, what the yard holds and any shortfall. Green means everything is there. If something is short you can still create the order, and Request the shortfall raises a requisition for exactly the missing quantity — so the last stage is never the first time anyone finds out.

    See

    One chassis number and one trailer record per unit are created immediately and reserved to this work order — open Serial Register and they are there, stage Material, status In Production. The number is now frozen: nobody can edit it. Cancelling the work order marks its trailers Scrapped but never reuses the numbers.

    Stops you

    "Chassis Numbering Rule not confirmed" — tick the box in step 4·1. An item without the Is a Trailer tick gets an ordinary work order with no chassis numbers, which is correct for parts.

  2. Emboss the plates

    Screen: Chassis Plates (also reachable from Serial Register)
    Do

    The screen lists every trailer still awaiting embossing, or one work order's batch. Press Print sheet for the fabrication bay. When the numbers have been struck into the plates, tick the trailers and press Mark embossed ("Record the embossing step against N trailer(s)?").

    See

    Each trailer's Plate column turns from Pending to Embossed, with who recorded it. Serial Register's Awaiting Embossing count falls.

  3. Move it through the six stages into stock

    Screen: Manufacturing · the last step posts the stock entry
    Do

    Open the work order on the Manufacturing screen. It shows the current stage and a progress percentage. Add a transition note if you like and press Move to Fabrication; then, as the floor progresses, Move to Assembly, Move to Paint, Move to QC, and finally Deliver & Add to Stock.

    Material→Fabrication→Assembly→Paint→QC→Delivery
    See

    Every move is written to the Stage History with the time, the person and the note. The last button does two things at once: it completes the work order and posts the finished trailers into the Finished Goods yard. The card then reads "Delivered — added to stock", each trailer's status becomes In Stock, and Live Stock lists them as identified chassis numbers, not as a quantity.

    Stops you

    Only the final stage puts anything in stock. A trailer at QC is still In Production and cannot be sold. "Work order is already at the final stage" means there is nothing left to move.

Sell

an order reserves a specific chassis; delivery and invoice carry it · 4 steps

  1. Take the customer's order

    Screen: Transactions › Sales Orders · creates SAL-ORD-2026-00011
    Do

    Press New Order, choose the customer, the trailer model, quantity and price, Save. Back on the list, press Confirm ("Confirm sales order …?").

    See

    The top of the screen shows Trailers in Stock — the finished units free to be sold. The order row shows its trailers once reserved, and its delivery progress.

  2. Reserve a trailer — or build one

    Screen: Transactions › Sales Orders
    Do

    Press Reserve: the screen lists the chassis numbers in stock and asks which one this customer gets. If nothing suitable is in stock, press Build instead: it asks for the trailer model and quantity and raises a work order for this order — which issues new chassis numbers straight away (step 6). When that work order reaches Deliver & Add to Stock, its trailers are reserved to this order by themselves; nobody has to come back and press Reserve.

    See

    The chosen (or newly built) trailer's status becomes Sold and its record shows Reserved For this order. It no longer appears as free stock, so two salespeople cannot promise the same unit. The Manufacturing screen shows which order a work order is for, and the traveler prints it.

    Stops you

    "Trailer … is In Production, so it cannot be reserved." Only In Stock units can be reserved. "No active BOM for …" means the model cannot be built until its parts list exists.

  3. Dispatch it — the delivery note

    Screen: Transactions › Delivery & Invoice · creates MAT-DN-2026-00008
    Do

    Confirmed orders wait under Awaiting Dispatch with their reserved chassis. Press Dispatch ("Dispatch … with 1 trailer(s)?"). A draft delivery note is made carrying the chassis number (several trailers are all named on it). Press Open, check it, and Submit when the trailer leaves the yard.

    See

    On submission the trailer's status becomes Delivered, its owner becomes the customer, and it leaves the stock figures. The row now offers Print (the delivery note, with the chassis on it) and Invoice.

  4. Invoice it

    Screen: Transactions › Delivery & Invoice · creates ACC-SINV-2026-00006
    Do

    Press Invoice ("Invoice delivery …?"). A draft sales invoice is made from the delivery note; the chassis number is carried onto it and prints on the invoice. Open it and Submit.

    See

    The invoice appears with its due date and Outstanding amount, and a Receive button. The customer is invoiced for a specific, named trailer.

    Stops you

    Delete (asks twice: "This cannot be undone") removes a draft invoice only. A submitted one "sits in the ledger — cancel it first"; that is the books' own rule, and the button is only shown to people whose role allows deletion.

Money and the books

the standard ledger — no separate books are kept · 3 steps

  1. Receive the customer's payment

    Screen: Transactions › Delivery & Invoice, then Payments & Receipts
    Do

    Press Receive on the invoice. It shows the outstanding amount and asks how much came in — full or part. A draft receipt is made. On Payments & Receipts, press Submit ("Submit …?") once the money is in the bank.

    See

    Owed to Us falls; the invoice's Outstanding falls to zero and it reads Paid. Every payment in or out is listed against what it settled.

  2. Journals, accruals and adjustments

    Screen: Transactions › Journal Entries
    Do

    For anything that is not a bill, invoice or payment — a month-end accrual, a correction, an opening balance — press New Journal, enter the account lines, Save. Back on the list the journal waits under Awaiting Approval until someone presses Post ("Post journal … to the ledger?").

    Stops you

    "Journal does not balance: debit X against credit Y" — the two sides must be equal. "A journal needs at least one line."

  3. Where the money stands

    Screens: Payments & Receipts, Supplier Payments, Live Stock, Home
    See

    Payments & Receipts opens with Owed to Us (customer receivables) and Owed by Us (supplier payables). Supplier Payments ages each supplier's balance. Live Stock shows Stock Value and Payments Missing (received material not yet billed or paid). Home shows this month's revenue and new customers. Everything you see here is the standard ledger — the same figures the accountant sees in the standard forms and reports; the portal keeps no books of its own.

6 · After the sale

Warranty

Each trailer's record (open it from Serial Register) carries a Warranty Start and Warranty Expiry date, and the current owner. Set the dates when the trailer is delivered; the register then shows every trailer's warranty position at a glance, years after the sale.

Repairs and servicing

This system covers trading and manufacturing only. Repair or service work on a sold trailer is not recorded here; if that is ever wanted, the workshop side of the platform can be added back as a separate step.

10 · Status words

A trailer

  • In Production — a work order has issued its number; it is on the floor, at one of the six stages.
  • In Stock — finished and in the Finished Goods yard; free to be reserved.
  • Sold — reserved against a customer's order; no longer free stock.
  • Delivered — the delivery note was submitted; the customer is now the owner.
  • Scrapped — its work order was cancelled. The number is never reused.

A document

  • Draft — saved, changeable, not yet counting.
  • Approved / Submitted — posted and final. Sub-statuses such as To Receive and Bill, Unpaid, Paid say what is still to happen.
  • On Hold — a purchase invoice parked before it reaches the books.
  • Cancelled — undone; an amended copy can be made.

A supplier

  • Clear — papers in date; orders allowed. Expiring — a paper lapses within 30 days. Blocked — orders refused.

11 · Common questions

Can I type the chassis number myself?
No. It is issued by the numbering rule when the work order is created, and frozen from then on. That is what makes it trustworthy.
What if a trailer is written off half-built?
Cancel the work order. Its trailers become Scrapped; their numbers stay on record and are never issued again.
Two customers want the same trailer.
Whoever reserves it first gets it; it disappears from the free list. The other order can Build a new one.
The material arrived but the bill has not.
Submit the goods receipt now — stock is real. Bill later from the receipt. Live Stock's Payments Missing shows receipts with no bill yet.
Where do I change something I already approved?
Open it in the standard form, Cancel, then Amend. A draft can simply be edited.
Why does the top of each list show numbers?
They are the live totals for that screen — how many are waiting, how much is outstanding — so you can see where you stand before scrolling.
Do the portal figures match the accountant's?
Yes. The portal reads the standard ledger; it keeps no separate books.
Old vehicle links?
The vehicle register of the workshop product is not part of this system. Old links to it land on the portal home.

13 · Words explained

Requisition
A request for material — "we need 20 sheets of steel". Not an order yet.
Purchase order
The order sent to a supplier. Approving it commits A3 to buy.
Goods receipt
The record that material arrived. Submitting it puts the stock in the yard.
Purchase invoice / bill
The supplier's bill. Approving it posts what A3 owes.
Ledger / the books
The accounts. "Posting to the ledger" means the figure now counts.
Work order
The instruction to build N trailers of a model, to stock or for a named customer order. Issues the chassis numbers.
Traveler
The printed work-order sheet the floor works from: specification, materials, operations, stage history.
BOM (parts list)
The recipe for one trailer: every item and operation that goes into it.
Chassis number
The trailer's permanent identity, embossed on its plate.
Serial
The trailer's record in the system; carries the chassis number, stage, status, owner and warranty.
Stage
Where a trailer is on the floor: Material, Fabrication, Assembly, Paint, QC, Delivery.
Yard / warehouse
Stores (raw material), Work In Progress (on the floor), Finished Goods (ready to sell).
Sales order
The customer's confirmed order. A specific trailer is reserved against it.
Delivery note
The paper that goes with the trailer out of the yard; carries the chassis.
Sales invoice
The customer's bill; carries the chassis; Receive records the money against it.
Payment / receipt
Money out to a supplier, or in from a customer, allocated against the bill it settles.
Journal
A manual accounting entry for anything that is not a bill, invoice or payment.
Draft / Submit / Cancel / Amend
Saved but not counting / posted and final / undone / a corrected copy of a cancelled one.
Standard form
The full entry form that opens when you press New or Open — the underlying ERPNext screen.
