A3 Trading Management · work instructions

What you do in the system, role by role

The system follows one trailer from the material that goes into it to the customer who drives it away. Every role owns one part of that road and hands the trailer to the next. Find your role below; each task is written as the buttons you press, in order.

Sign in at the portal address your administrator gave youEverything you need is on the left-hand menuVersion of 23 Sep 2026

Before you start: three things everyone needs to know

1. Draft, then approved. When you save something it is a Draft — you can still change it and it does not count yet. When it is approved (the standard forms call the same button Submit) it is final and it counts: stock moves, money is owed. If you need to change an approved document, it has to be cancelled first. So check before you approve.

2. Two kinds of screen. The portal screens on the left-hand menu are where you work all day. When you press New … or Open, the full entry form opens in a new tab — fill it in, press Save (Ctrl+S), and if the task says so press Submit then Yes. Close that tab and refresh the portal list; your document is there with its number.

3. Write the number down. Every document has a number — PUR-ORD-2026-00023, MFG-WO-2026-00018, chassis A3-26-000304. When you hand work to the next person, hand them the number.

Role Purchase clerk

Transactions › RequisitionsTransactions › Purchase OrdersSuppliers
Every morning
  • Open Requisitions: anything under Awaiting Approval is a request from Stores or Production waiting on you.
  • Open Suppliers: the banner tells you if a supplier's papers have lapsed. Chase the papers before you need to order from them.

Task 1 — Approve or reject a request for material

When a requisition appears as Draft.
  1. Read the line: item, quantity, which yard it should go to.
  2. Approve → confirm. Or Reject and type the reason — the requester sees it.

Task 2 — Turn an approved request into an order

  1. Press To Order on the approved requisition. The box lists only suppliers whose papers are in order — type the one you want.
  2. Go to Purchase Orders. The new order is a Draft. Press Open and check the price on each line (a request carries no price; the order takes the item's buying price — if it shows 0, type the agreed rate). Save.
  3. Back on the list, Approve → confirm. The order is now with the supplier; Order Value at the top includes it.
If the system refuses: "Supplier not compliant — lapsed: trade licence …" means that supplier's papers have expired. Get the new papers, update the dates on the supplier (Suppliers › Open), then order.
Hand-off → Stores, with the order number, so they know what is coming.

Task 3 — An order without a request

When you buy something nobody requisitioned.
  1. Purchase Orders › New Order → supplier, items, quantities, rates → Save → back on the list, Approve.

Task 4 — Keep supplier papers current

  1. Suppliers › Open the supplier → enter the expiry dates of the trade licence, VAT certificate and establishment card, set the compliance status → Save. Clear means orders allowed; Expiring means a paper lapses within 30 days; Blocked means orders refused.

Role Storekeeper

Transactions › Goods ReceiptLive StockTransactions › Requisitions
Every morning
  • Open Live Stock: Low Stock Alerts is your shopping list; Payments Missing is material received that Accounts has not been billed for yet.
  • Open Goods Receipt: Awaiting Receipt lists the orders due in.

Task 1 — Receive material against an order

When the truck arrives.
  1. On Goods Receipt, find the order under Awaiting Receipt and press Receive → confirm. A Draft receipt is made with the ordered quantities.
  2. Count what actually came. If it differs, press Edit, correct the quantities (or mark rejected ones), Save.
  3. When the material is physically in the yard, press Submit → confirm "This posts the stock". Not before — this is the moment the yard's figure changes.
Hand-off → Accounts: press Bill on the receipt when the supplier's invoice arrives, or tell Accounts the receipt number so they can.

Task 2 — Ask for what is running low

  1. On Live Stock, tick the rows under Low Stock Alerts that need ordering.
  2. Press Request Selected. One requisition is raised for all of them; items already on an open request are skipped.
Hand-off → Purchase, who approve and order (their Task 1 and 2).

Role Production supervisor

ManufacturingSerial RegisterChassis Plates
Every morning
  • Open Home: the Production Board shows every open work order with its stage and progress; Trailers by Stage shows where the floor is.
  • Open Manufacturing and walk the list: anything that moved on the floor yesterday gets its stage moved today.

Task 1 — Raise a work order

When Sales asks for trailers to be built for an order, or when stock of a model is low.
  1. Manufacturing › New.
  2. Search the trailer model. Its parts list (BOM) is selected for you.
  3. Set the quantity. Read the material check that appears: green means the yard holds everything; a warning lists what is short. If short, you may still create the order — press Request the shortfall so Stores and Purchase get a requisition for exactly the missing quantity.
  4. Planned start and target delivery dates.
  5. "Take raw material from" — Stores. "Finished trailers go to" — Finished Goods. Leave these unless told otherwise.
  6. "For sales order": if a customer has already ordered these trailers, pick their order — the finished trailers will then go to that order by themselves. Otherwise leave Build to stock.
  7. Write the specification for the floor — colour, axles, options. It prints on the traveler.
  8. Create Work Order. The chassis numbers are issued at this moment, one per trailer. Open Serial Register and you will see them, status In Production.
If the system refuses: "Chassis Numbering Rule not confirmed" — the chassis format has not been confirmed by management; nothing can be built until it is. "No active BOM found" — the model has no parts list yet.
Hand-off → Fabrication bay: print the traveler (Print on the order) and the plate sheet (their Task 1).

Task 2 — Move the order through the stages

Each time the floor finishes a stage.
  1. On Manufacturing, select the order. The button names the next stage: Move to Fabrication, then Assembly, Paint, QC.
  2. Type a note if there is something worth recording (who, what was done, a problem) — it goes into the Stage History with the time and your name.
  3. Press the button. Do this on the day it happens; the history is the record.

Task 3 — Finish: put the trailers into stock

When QC has passed.
  1. Press Deliver & Add to Stock. Two things happen at once: the raw material is taken out of the yard, and the finished trailers appear in Finished Goods — as chassis numbers, not a quantity.
  2. Check the card reads "Delivered — added to stock". If it instead shows "Stock not posted: …", the yard was short of material: get the material in (Stores) and press the button again.
  3. On Serial Register the trailers now read In Stock — or Sold if the order was built for a customer, already reserved to them.
Hand-off → Sales: the trailers are ready to reserve or dispatch.

Role Fabrication bay

Chassis Platesthe printed traveler

Task 1 — Emboss the plates

  1. Open Chassis Plates. Every trailer still waiting for its plate is listed with its chassis number. Press Print sheet.
  2. Strike the numbers into the plates exactly as printed — the number cannot be changed afterwards.
  3. Tick the trailers you have done and press Mark embossed → confirm. Their Plate column reads Embossed, with your name.

Task 2 — Work to the traveler

  1. The traveler (printed by the supervisor) carries the specification, the materials, the operations and the stage history. Tell the supervisor when a stage is finished so they move it in the system.

Role Sales

CustomersTransactions › Sales OrdersTransactions › Delivery & InvoiceSerial Register
Every morning
  • Open Sales Orders: Trailers in Stock tells you what you can promise today.
  • Open Delivery & Invoice: Awaiting Dispatch is what is ready to go; Outstanding is what customers still owe.

Task 1 — Take an order

  1. If the customer is new: Customers › Add Customer → name, phone, email → Save.
  2. Sales Orders › New Order → customer, trailer model, quantity, price, delivery date → Save.
  3. Back on the list, Confirm → confirm. The order is now firm.

Task 2 — Give the customer a trailer: reserve or build

  1. If a suitable trailer is in stock: Reserve. The box lists every chassis in stock; type the serial id of the one this customer gets. It becomes Sold and disappears from the free list — nobody else can promise it.
  2. If nothing suitable is in stock: Build. Type the model and quantity. A work order is raised for this order and Production takes over. When they finish, the trailers are reserved to your order automatically — you will see the chassis on the order row.
Hand-off → Production (if built). Tell them the order number and the specification the customer wants.

Task 3 — Dispatch

When the trailer leaves the yard.
  1. Delivery & Invoice: under Awaiting Dispatch, press Dispatch on the order → confirm. A Draft delivery note is made carrying the chassis number.
  2. Press Open, check the customer and the chassis, then Submit → Yes. The trailer now reads Delivered with the customer as owner.
  3. Print the delivery note for the driver — the chassis number is on it.

Task 4 — Invoice and record the money

  1. On the delivery note row, press Invoice → confirm. A Draft invoice is made with the chassis on it. Open → Submit → Yes.
  2. When the customer pays, press Receive on the invoice, accept the amount (or type a part payment). Accounts submits the receipt (their Task 3).
Do not use Delete on a submitted invoice — the system refuses; a posted invoice must be cancelled by Accounts. Delete only removes a draft.

Task 5 — Warranty

  1. On Serial Register, open the delivered trailer's record and set Warranty Start (delivery date) and Warranty Expiry. The register then shows every trailer's warranty position for as long as A3 keeps records.

Role Accounts

Transactions › Purchase InvoicesTransactions › Supplier PaymentsTransactions › Payments & ReceiptsTransactions › Journal Entries
Every morning
  • Payments & Receipts: Owed to Us and Owed by Us are the two numbers that matter.
  • Purchase Invoices: anything On Hold is waiting for a decision; anything in Draft is waiting for approval.
  • Supplier Payments: the balance by age shows who has waited longest.

Task 1 — Approve a supplier's bill (or hold it)

  1. A bill arrives as a Draft after Stores pressed Bill on the receipt (or press Bill yourself on Goods Receipt).
  2. Compare it with the receipt and the order. If something is wrong, press Hold and type the reason — the bill cannot enter the books while held.
  3. When it is right: Release (if held), then Approve → confirm "This posts it to the ledger". The amount is now owed.

Task 2 — Pay a supplier

  1. On the approved bill, press Pay. Accept the outstanding amount or type a part payment. A Draft payment is made.
  2. On Supplier Payments, once the money has actually left the bank, press Submit → confirm. The supplier's balance falls; the bill reads Paid.

Task 3 — Record a customer's payment

  1. Sales press Receive on the invoice; you find the Draft receipt on Payments & Receipts and press Submit once the money is in the bank. Owed to Us falls; the invoice reads Paid.

Task 4 — Journals

  1. For anything that is not a bill, invoice or payment (a correction, an accrual, an opening balance): Journal Entries › New Journal → account lines → Save. Debits must equal credits or it will not save.
  2. Back on the list, Post → confirm.

Role Owner / manager

HomeSerial RegisterLive Stockevery Transactions screen
Every morning, one screen
  • Home: trailers in stock and in production, deliveries and revenue this month, money owed each way, the production board, recent deliveries, low stock. If a number looks wrong, the link on each card takes you to the screen behind it.

The decisions only you make

  1. The chassis format. Once, before the first trailer: set the format in Chassis Numbering Rule and tick Format Confirmed. Nothing can be built until you do. Never change the format after the first number is issued.
  2. Bills on hold. Accounts hold a bill when something is wrong; you decide whether it is released.
  3. Cancellations. A posted document (invoice, delivery note, work order) is undone only by cancelling it in the standard form. Cancelling a work order scraps its trailers; their chassis numbers are never reused.
