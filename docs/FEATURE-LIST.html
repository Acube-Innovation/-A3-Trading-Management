1The system in one page

A3 buys material from suppliers, builds trailers from it, gives every trailer its own chassis number, holds finished trailers in stock, sells them to customers, delivers and invoices them, and keeps the warranty on record afterwards. A3 Trading Management runs that whole business on ERPNext, through a portal made for daily use.

The central idea: a trailer is an individual, not a quantity. The moment A3 decides to build one, it receives a chassis number, and that number follows it through the whole system: the plate in the fabrication bay, the stock list, the sales reservation, the delivery note, the invoice and the warranty record. A3 never sells "3 flatbeds"; it sells chassis A3-26-000296.

Main capabilities

  • Buying: requisitions, purchase orders, goods receipt, supplier bills with an approval hold, supplier payments.
  • Supplier compliance: licence, VAT and establishment expiry tracking; orders to lapsed suppliers are refused.
  • Manufacturing: work orders with a material check, build to stock or to a customer order, six tracked production stages and a printed traveler.
  • Chassis and serial control: chassis numbers issued automatically from a configurable format, frozen once issued, never reused.
  • Selling: sales orders that reserve a specific chassis, build-to-order, dispatch and invoicing that carry the chassis number.
  • Accounts: customer receipts, supplier payments, journal entries, receivables and payables — all on the standard ledger.
  • Live Stock: stock value, stock by warehouse, low-stock alerts with one-click requisitions, trailers listed by chassis.
  • Dashboard: trailers in stock and in production, deliveries, revenue and money owed each way.

No separate books

Every portal screen is a view onto standard ERPNext documents. Approving a purchase invoice in the portal submits the real Purchase Invoice; posting a journal submits the real Journal Entry. The figures on the portal are the same figures the accountant sees in ERPNext's own reports.

Draft first, then approved

Almost every document has two states. Saved, it is a draft: it can still be changed or deleted and has no effect on stock or money. Approved (ERPNext calls this Submit), it is posted: stock moves, money is owed, and it can no longer be edited. A posted document is undone by cancelling it and, if needed, amending a copy. Nothing counts until a person approves it.

2How a trailer moves through the system

The whole business is one chain. Each link is a screen in the portal, and each hands its result to the next.

BuildA work order issues the chassis numbers
StagesThe trailer moves through six production stages
StockThe finished trailer enters the Finished Goods yard
SellA sales order reserves a specific chassis
DeliverDelivery note and invoice carry the chassis
WarrantyStart and expiry held on the same number

The full cycle, step by step

#StepScreenWhat happens
1Request materialRequisitionsA requisition lists the items and quantities needed and the yard they go to. It is approved, rejected, or turned into a purchase order.
2Order itPurchase OrdersThe order is checked (prices, supplier compliance) and approved. It now waits to be received and billed.
3Receive itGoods ReceiptA receipt is drafted with the ordered quantities, corrected if less arrived, and submitted. The stock is now in the Stores yard.
4Get the billPurchase InvoicesThe supplier's bill is raised from the receipt. It can be put on hold with a reason, released, then approved into the ledger.
5Pay the supplierSupplier PaymentsA full or part payment is drafted against the bill and submitted when the money goes.
6Raise the work orderManufacturingThe system checks material availability, then issues one chassis number and one trailer record per unit, reserved to the work order.
7Emboss the platesChassis PlatesAn embossing sheet is printed for the fabrication bay; once struck, the plates are marked embossed.
8Build through six stagesManufacturingThe work order moves Material → Fabrication → Assembly → Paint → QC → Delivery. The last move completes it and posts the trailers into Finished Goods.
9Take the customer's orderSales OrdersA sales order is created and confirmed.
10Reserve or build a trailerSales OrdersA specific in-stock chassis is reserved to the order, or a new work order is raised for it (build to order).
11Dispatch itDelivery & InvoiceA delivery note carrying the chassis is created and submitted when the trailer leaves the yard. The customer becomes its owner.
12Invoice itDelivery & InvoiceThe sales invoice is made from the delivery note and carries the chassis number.
13Receive paymentPayments & ReceiptsA full or part receipt is recorded against the invoice.
14WarrantySerial RegisterWarranty start and expiry dates are held on the trailer's record for life.

This same chain is automated as an end-to-end test that posts every document in order and fails loudly if any link breaks.

Common features of every portal screen

  • Summary figures at the top — live totals for that screen (how many are waiting, how much is outstanding) so you see where you stand before scrolling.
  • Search and filters on every list, and a count of the records shown.
  • Action buttons on each row that move a document to its next step (Approve, Receive, Bill, Pay, Reserve, Dispatch, Invoice…). Each asks for confirmation before it posts anything.
  • New / Open opens the full standard ERPNext form in a new tab, where every field is available. Save, close the tab, and the document appears on the portal list.
  • Clear refusals — when a rule stops an action, the screen says why and what to do (see section 14).
  • Role selection — a user with more than one role can choose how they want to use the system.
  • Old links are redirected — addresses of screens from the earlier workshop product land on the portal home instead of failing.

6Suppliers and compliance

A3 may only buy from suppliers whose papers are in date. The system tracks them and enforces it.

Suppliers screen

Menu › Suppliers
  • Supplier list with contact details, balances and compliance position.
  • Add and edit suppliers, including their compliance papers.
  • Each supplier shows one of three positions: Clear papers in date, orders allowed · Expiring a paper lapses within 30 days · Blocked orders refused.

Compliance data held on each supplier

  • Trade licence expiry date
  • VAT certificate expiry date and whether VAT input is eligible
  • Establishment card expiry date
  • Compliance status, compliance score and country
  • Whether non-compliance blocks purchase orders
Enforcement: every purchase order is checked when it is saved. If any paper has lapsed, or the status is not compliant, the order is refused with the exact reason, e.g. "Purchase Order cannot be raised for <supplier>: lapsed: trade licence …".

Where trailers are built. A work order is the instruction to build a number of trailers of one model, either to stock or for a named customer order. Raising it is also the moment chassis numbers are issued.

Material check and shortfall request

As soon as the BOM and quantity are set, the form lists every raw material with what is needed, what the yard holds, and any shortfall. Green means everything is there. The order can still be created when something is short, and Request the shortfall raises a requisition for exactly the missing quantities — so the shortage is found at the start, not at the last stage.

Six production stages

Material→Fabrication→Assembly→Paint→QC→Delivery
  • Open a work order to see its current stage and a progress percentage.
  • Move to Fabrication / Assembly / Paint / QC advances one stage, with an optional transition note.
  • Deliver & Add to Stock is the last move: it completes the work order and posts the Manufacture stock entry that puts the finished trailers into Finished Goods. The card then reads "Delivered — added to stock".
  • Stage History records every move with time, person and note.
  • Each trailer's record mirrors its work order's stage automatically.
  • Only the final stage puts anything into stock; a trailer at QC is still In Production and cannot be sold.

Production detail and costing

  • Raw material lines with rate and amount.
  • Operations with workstation, minutes and hourly rate.
  • Operating cost, total production cost and cost per unit.
  • Work-in-progress value across open orders.

Work order traveler (print)

  • The printed sheet the floor works from.
  • Work order, quantity, planned start, target delivery, sales order, yards.
  • Specification, raw materials, operations.
  • Stage table with "Done By / Sign" boxes, stage history, and sign-off for production supervisor, quality check and store / dispatch.

Build to order

When a work order is linked to a sales order — chosen on the form, or raised with Build from the Sales Orders screen — its finished trailers are reserved to that order automatically when they reach stock (never more than the order's quantity). Nobody has to come back and reserve them.

8Chassis numbers and serial control

Each physical trailer has one Trailer Serial record carrying its chassis number for life.

Chassis Numbering Rule

Standard form › Chassis Numbering Rule (one setting for the company)
SettingMeaning
PrefixLeading letters, e.g. A3.
Include year / year formatWhether the year appears, as 26 or 2026.
SeparatorCharacter between parts, e.g. -.
Sequence length / next numberDigits in the running number and the number to start from.
Check digitNone, Mod 10 (Luhn) or Mod 11.
Next chassis numberA live preview of the next number, e.g. A3-26-000297.
Format ConfirmedMust be ticked before any chassis number is issued.
The number is embossed into steel and cannot be taken back, so the system never guesses a format: until the format is confirmed, trailer work orders are refused.

Automatic issue

  • A work order for N trailers issues N chassis numbers and N trailer records at the moment it is created, reserved to that work order — whether raised from the portal, the standard form or an import.
  • Only items ticked Is a Trailer receive chassis numbers; parts and raw material get ordinary work orders.
  • Once issued, the chassis number is frozen — nobody can edit it.
  • Cancelling a work order marks its trailers Scrapped but keeps the numbers on record; they are never reissued, so no two trailers can share a number.

What each trailer record holds

  • Serial number and chassis number
  • Trailer type (model)
  • Status and production stage
  • Originating work order
  • Current warehouse and company
  • Sales order it is reserved for
  • Current owner (customer) and name
  • Warranty start and expiry
  • Embossed flag, embossed on, embossed by

Serial Register

Menu › Serial Register
  • Every chassis A3 has ever issued.
  • Search by chassis, serial, model, work order, yard or owner.
  • Filter by stage and status.
  • Shows plate (Pending / Embossed) and warranty position.
  • Summary counts, including Awaiting Embossing.

Chassis Plates

Menu › Chassis Plates
  • Lists trailers awaiting embossing, or one work order's batch.
  • Print sheet for the fabrication bay.
  • Mark embossed on the ticked trailers records who did it and when; Plate turns from Pending to Embossed.

Chassis number on selling documents

Sales orders, delivery notes and sales invoices carry the trailer serial and chassis number on each line, and the chassis prints on the delivery note and invoice.

14Built-in rules and safeguards

These refusals are deliberate. Each protects something that would be expensive to get wrong.

When you seeIt meansWhat to do
Chassis Numbering Rule not confirmedNobody has confirmed the chassis format, so no number will be issued.Set the format and tick Format Confirmed.
Supplier not compliantTrade licence, VAT certificate or establishment card has lapsed, or status is not compliant.Update the supplier's papers and status.
… is on hold. Release the hold before approving it.The purchase invoice was parked with a reason.Resolve it, Release, then Approve.
… has nothing outstanding to payThe bill totals zero, usually because the order had no prices.Put prices on the order before billing.
Trailer … is In Production, so it cannot be reservedOnly finished trailers in stock can be sold.Wait for Deliver & Add to Stock, or Build.
No active BOM for …The model has no parts list, so it cannot be built.Create and activate the BOM.
Work order is already at the final stageThe trailers are already in stock.Nothing to do.
… is submitted and sits in the ledger. Cancel it firstA posted invoice cannot simply be deleted.Cancel it in the standard form first.
Journal does not balanceDebits and credits differ.Correct the lines so both sides are equal.

Other protections

  • Chassis numbers cannot be typed or edited, and are never reused.
  • A reserved trailer disappears from free stock at once.
  • Every posting action asks for confirmation first; deleting an invoice asks twice.
  • Nothing affects stock or money until it is approved.

18Scope: what is not included

  • Workshop service: repairs and servicing of sold trailers, job cards, estimates, appointments, telecalling, complaints and inspections are outside this system. It covers trading and manufacturing only.
  • Vehicle and driver records of the earlier workshop product are not part of this system.
  • Loading of historical data.
  • New third-party integrations.
  • A native mobile app (the portal works in a browser).
  • Payroll and HR.
